## 1. Positioning & Strategy

**Who the site is for.** Four buyer groups along the elevator value chain, DACH-first: (1) control- and component manufacturers whose customers' technicians depend on their manuals and free hotlines (Ziehl-Abegg, Meiller, Schaefer, CEDES type — the NEW LIFT mirror); (2) spare-parts distributors and parts desks drowning in unstructured email/photo quote requests (FLP/elevator-store type — the ALGI mirror); (3) the ~800 independent German service SMEs fighting the Fachkräftemangel with mixed multi-manufacturer fleets; (4) elevator manufacturers combining both patterns. Multiplier audience without its own page: consultancies and lift-management firms (HUNDT-CONSULT/Jappsen type) sitting on norms, inspection and fleet-history corpora — captured via nav copy, the demo role dropdown and one Wissensagent mention. Secondary audience: technical evaluators and developers who arrive via GitHub and the open core.

**The category claim.** *Die KI-Plattform für die Aufzugsbranche / The AI platform for the elevator industry.* The claim is verifiably uncontested: every credible elevator-AI story today is OEM-internal (TKE/Microsoft, KONE's assistant, Schindler Ahead, Otis/WeMaintain) or in-house (Kollmorgen's K-Solutions bot). Nobody sells this capability to component makers, distributors and independents — and Exulu holds the only two named production references in the segment.

**Why Exulu wins.**
- **Proof, not promises:** NEW LIFT (Newton: ~4,500 technical handbooks often 400+ pages, 2,000+ Zendesk tickets, 50,000+ legacy service cases, 7 knowledge bases, live-demoed at interlift 2025, agent integrated into the control units) and ALGI (email inbox → daily ERP CSV parts import → customer-specific price lists → offer created in ABAS ERP via API). *ALGI is a confirmed named reference (routine sign-offs on final copy, metric and quote — Section 10 #3); NEW LIFT's non-public facts remain approval-gated — see the dependency matrix in Section 10.*
- **Engineering the industry can verify:** deterministic 4-phase agentic retrieval, chunk/page-level citations, separator-insensitive part-number search (FST2XT finds FST_2XT), product-generation disambiguation (FST-2XT answers never come from the FST2 manual), eval framework with LLM-as-judge — published as a scoreboard, not an adjective (see /plattform/agentic-retrieval).
- **Sovereignty no US SaaS can match, stated precisely:** every deployment model is 100% EU. The default for most customers is Exulu's fully managed platform-as-a-service on Google Cloud (EU regions) or Hetzner, operated to the highest security standards; self-hosting on the customer's own infrastructure is the option for those who want it (proven: EU-only cloud deployment via Terraform) — up to fully on-prem with open-weight models. Model calls only through EU-pinned endpoints controlled in the gateway; source-available core (Elastic License 2.0), GDPR tooling. *Never the falsifiable absolute "your data never leaves the building" — managed and reference deployments call EU model endpoints.*
- **Transparent pricing with a commercial USP:** Core free & source-available; Enterprise from EUR 900/month (annual) / EUR 1,200/month (monthly) — **one flat fee for the whole company, no per-seat pricing**; tokens at provider prices through the customer's own gateway, governed by budgets per user/role/team/project/agent with auto-blocking. Per-seat generic AI (ChatGPT/Claude/Copilot business and pro tiers) scales linearly with headcount and knows nothing about handbooks, tickets or ERPs — at 30–50 seats the subscriptions alone overtake the IMP license. None of the benchmarked high-ACV AI vendors publish prices at all.
- **Multilingual by design:** every researched target ships documentation in 5–18 languages but supports in one or two. German/English source docs in, answers in the technician's or distributor's language out — a first-class message, not a sub-bullet.

**Strategic bets accepted:** all-in vertical (the platform page + GitHub keep the horizontal door open with "Weitere Branchen — sprechen Sie uns an" nav copy); depth-per-page over sprawl; German at the root domain; approval sequencing before copywriting — every consent-dependent surface has a defined approval-independent fallback (Section 10).
