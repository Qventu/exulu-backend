Platform Release · 22 June 2026

Exulu, made calm.

One workspace that shows you only what your role needs — and gets you there in a single click. The whole product was rebuilt to feel like one thing: a calm command center that adapts to who just logged in, works the same on a phone as on a 1440-px display, and is honest about what the AI is doing underneath. Nothing was removed — everything just found its place.

01 — Navigation

The Spine

Calm, role-aware navigation — a focused app for end-users, a grouped command center for admins, and a purple indicator that always shows where you are.

Navigation was rebuilt around a single idea: show people only what their role needs. The new sidebar — the Spine — groups every surface by job (Workspace, Build, Develop, Administration) and trims itself to each person's permissions. A pure end-user opens a focused four-item app; an admin gets a calm, grouped command center. Same shell, composed per role.

The purple indicator is the through-line. It slides with you as you move between surfaces, so "where am I" is never a question. Hover and active states stay quiet — one accent does the work, no purple confetti.

Nothing was hidden or removed. Every capability simply found its place on a clear hierarchy, one deliberate click away — with a ⌘K command palette for the rest.

02 — Responsive

Mobile, for real

13 screens used to render nothing on a phone. Now every surface passes at 390px — chat goes full-screen, tables become cards, and the nav is one tap away.

A 390-px phone frame: the Today dashboard reordered triage-first, a tap on the hamburger slides the nav drawer in, and a tap on Chat drops into a full-screen conversation with a thumb-reachable composer. Both themes, first-class.

03 — Design system

One design system

Three font weights replaced one chaotic scale, "informational" stopped rendering purple, and 53 files of hand-picked greens and reds became real semantic tokens — in both themes.

A before/after flip: the old font-black editorial hero and a mistakenly-purple "info" badge give way to one calm type scale, a genuinely blue --info, and unified success/warning/info tokens — proven with a quick light↔dark swap.

04 — Home

Role-composed Home

Your homepage is built from your role — triage first: what needs attention, what to resume, the numbers that matter.

The Today page assembles region by region for the persona signing in — Needs attention, then Resume, then a 2×2 grid of the vitals that matter — so the first screen is always the next action, not a wall of widgets.
