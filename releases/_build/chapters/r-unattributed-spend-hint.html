Spend, fully accounted

The gap tagged totals miss, now on the card.

Cost & governance

Dashboards count user-attributed LLM requests — so health checks, background jobs, and untagged calls were real spend with no explanation. Now, whenever that unattributed gap exceeds 1% of total spend, a quiet second caption appears on the Spend and Tokens cards on Home and across the analytics KPIs: "+ $12.34 unattributed". Trivial and negative gaps stay hidden. Your cards now add up to your bill.
